Wednesday 11 November 2026

Nvidia's board met on Wednesday to decide whether to remove CEO Jensen Huang, the Wall Street Journal and Reuters reported, after he admitted to two extramarital affairs, one with a woman US officials called a Chinese agent. Shares fell about 8 percent, erasing roughly 450 billion dollars, and Fortune noted millions of retirement accounts hold the stock.

In Abu Dhabi, Russia and Ukraine held their first peace talks in nine months, Reuters reported, with Trump envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff pressing both sides. Zelensky was absent and called Wednesday the main day of negotiations.

At the COP31 summit in Antalya, Turkey, host president Erdogan celebrated two new oil fields, drawing criticism as delegates from 190 nations met, AFP said. Brazil presented a roadmap to move economies off fossil fuels, the AP reported, and Greta Thunberg attended her first climate summit since 2021.

In Washington, House Republicans blocked impeachment of Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth on a 211 to 204 party line vote, the AP said. Bernie Sanders and Senator Elissa Slotkin said they will not back Chuck Schumer for Senate majority leader, and House Republicans elected Jim Jordan as their leader after midterm losses.

Israeli forces killed at least eight Palestinians in Gaza despite the US brokered ceasefire, where UNICEF warned flooded tents and broken sewage networks threaten children, and soldiers killed a 14 year old boy in the West Bank, Democracy Now reported.

The US government has taken more than 12 billion dollars from Venezuelan oil exports since President Maduro was removed in January, a KPMG report reviewed by the New York Times found. Separately, a US soldier was arrested in Britain for allegedly trying to pass NATO secrets to Russia.

In Jakarta, thousands of students rallied over the government's weak response to forest fires, demanding a national disaster declaration.
